# Billboard Chart Database

A local, hand-entered record of historical Billboard charts, transcribed week by week
from the printed magazine.

## Charts and weeks

**Chart**:
One ranked list Billboard printed every week under a lineage of names (e.g. Country
Jukebox), identified by its category and method.
_Avoid_: List, table

**Chart era**:
A stretch of time during which a chart was printed under one name and one size.

**Chart week**:
One chart as printed for one week-ending date: every chart entry on that chart for that
Saturday.
_Avoid_: Week (alone, when the chart matters), issue

**Previous chart**:
The chart week published immediately before a given one: the previous publication date,
whether or not it has been entered yet.
_Avoid_: Previous week on file, last entered week

**Chart entry**:
One recording at one position on one chart week.
_Avoid_: Row, listing

**Tie**:
Two or more chart entries sharing one position in the same chart week, as printed.

## Movement

**Last-week number**:
The previous-chart position printed next to a chart entry in the magazine.
_Avoid_: Last week (alone), previous position

**Carryover**:
A chart entry whose recording was also on the previous chart.

**Debut**:
A chart entry whose recording has never been on that chart before.
_Avoid_: New entry

**Re-entry**:
A chart entry whose recording was on that chart before, but not on the previous chart.

## Records and credits

**Recording**:
One side of a release: a song as issued on a specific label, catalog number and side.
_Avoid_: Record, single, track

**Release**:
One physical issue, identified by label and catalog number, carrying one or more sides.
Corresponds to a Discogs release.
_Avoid_: Disc, record, single

**Side**:
The face of a release a recording occupies: A, B, or AB for a song spanning both.

**Flip-side**:
Another recording on the same release as a given recording.
_Avoid_: Other side, B-side (when the given recording may itself be the B)

**Reissue**:
A recording re-released under a new label or catalog number, identical to an earlier one.

**Credit text**:
The artist billing exactly as printed on the chart.
_Avoid_: Artist field

**Artist**:
A canonical act, credited on recordings through recording credits.
_Avoid_: Performer, act name

**Alias**:
Another observed spelling or billing of an artist.

**Printed anomaly**:
A chart entry the magazine printed in a way the data can't otherwise represent, kept as
printed with a note.
